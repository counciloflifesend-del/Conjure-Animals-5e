Quick Start

The engine is designed to resolve the messy math involved when commanding a pack of summoned beasts.

1. Select Beast

Choose from the library or use custom stats on the calculator.

2. Set Quantity

Use the standard 1, 2, 4, or 8 creature breakpoints, or scale up for higher slots.

3. Target AC

Set the enemy armor class to calculate hit and crit probability.

math

Engine Mechanics

Natural 1 misses, natural 20 crits, and critical hits double the dice while keeping flat damage modifiers unchanged.

Expected damage is attacks multiplied by the probability-weighted average of normal hits and critical hits.

Beast Library Integration

The Beast Library is pre-loaded with common summons categorized by Challenge Rating and environment.

Importing a beast populates attack bonus, damage dice, creature count, and relevant traits. You can still override any field manually afterward.
